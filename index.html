Does the clinical Bernoulli equation overestimate aortic stenosis?

Doctors grade aortic stenosis with ΔP = 4·Vmax². I tested it against 3D CFD of four bicuspid valves, at flow rates from 5 to 30 L/min.

+34% to +50%Bernoulli overestimate on case 1, from 5 to 30 L/min
29.9 → 25.4mmHg, CFD pressure drop vs. mesh refinement stays under 0.35%
  • Measured valve area and severity by image processing in MATLAB on a cross-section at the vena contracta.
  • Repaired leaky CAD geometry with the Surface Wrapper; built polyhedral meshes with prism layers (y⁺ max 2.87); validated with a three-level GCI study.
  • Used mass-flow-weighted surface integrals, because a plain average of velocity underestimates kinetic energy in a non-uniform jet.
  • Showed the gap grows with flow because pressure recovery scales with ½ρV²; computed entropy production to quantify irreversible losses. The rheumatic valve dissipated most (7.17×10⁻³ W/K).

Result: internship report, defense, and a scientific paper in preparation, co-written with an experimental intern.

Where does smoke go in a corridor fire?

Natural convection and air/CO₂ transport in a 20 × 1.5 × 2.5 m corridor, built up one physics at a time: geometry, three meshes, thermal plume, then species.

1.45 mneutral plane height; air below 1.40 m stays breathable
2.2%fine vs. medium mesh difference

  • Limits noted: oscillating residuals from real plume instability, and about 100 s simulated, short of steady state.

Simulating a portfolio with fees, taxes and volatility

Dynamics of Abrikosov vortices in a superconductor

Numerical study of an N-body ODE system describing how quantum vortices repel each other and are pulled toward the center by magnetic confinement.

  • Wrote an explicit Euler solver in C++ with input files, separate headers and sources, and a build system.
  • Validated it against the known exact solutions (single vortex with three confinement laws, two-vortex cases), measured the convergence order, and tested stability as the time step grows.
  • Analyzed the observed behavior with figures and quantitative measures.

Friction between bike and kart tires on varied surfaces

How does the friction coefficient change with tire and surface? I built a pulley-and-mass rig and measured static friction by adding mass until sliding, then dynamic friction by two methods, with uncertainties from Monte Carlo.

0.93static μ, smooth kart tire on dry track
0.12 to 0.20static μ on ice, for the kart tires
  • Tested a rain kart tire, a smooth kart tire and a mountain-bike tire on wood, simulated asphalt, wet and icy tracks.
  • Explained the unexpectedly high wet grip with surface tension, and showed it by adding dish soap (μ fell from 0.7 to 0.3).
  • Dynamic friction fell from 0.51 to 0.17 as tire pressure rose from 1 to 4 bar.
